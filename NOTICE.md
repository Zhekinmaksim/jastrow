## The engraving

`web/assets/jastrow-1899.webp`

The duck-rabbit as printed in *Fliegende Blatter*, Munich, 23 October 1892,
page 147, unattributed. Published 134 years ago with no named author, so it is
in the public domain worldwide. Joseph Jastrow reproduced it in "The Mind's
Eye" in 1899, which is where this project takes its name.

`web/favicon.svg` and `web/assets/favicon-*.png` are an original schematic
redraw of the same figure, made for this project, and fall under LICENSE.

the upstream Google Fonts releases.
