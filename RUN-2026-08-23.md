accept any well-formed answer. The current `prompt_comparative` construction
therefore cannot support the Jastrow measurement.

## Non-comparative gate status

The official SDK documents `prompt_non_comparative` as leader task execution
followed by validator integrity checking against explicit criteria. A minimal
gate in `contracts/non_comparative_probe.py` uses criteria limited to JSON,
schema and vocabulary validity and explicitly forbids re-judging the answer.

Its deploy settled unanimously. Three attempted probe submissions did not
create GenLayer transactions: two EVM submissions reverted and one was refused
with RPC `-32005 node at capacity`. Bradbury simultaneously showed 159
transactions in `proposing`, and another Jastrow diagnostic probe remained in
`proposing` with all selected validators `not_voted`. The non-comparative gate
is therefore inconclusive, not failed.

## Stop condition

Do not run the battery, embed a report, publish `web/`, fill battery costs or
re-render the reel until one of these is true:

1. five non-comparative probes settle and record at least two different valid
   answers; or
2. the design is replaced with a mechanism that does not depend on validators
   accepting a leader answer they disagree with.
